3. OCR bilan nimalar qilsak boladi?

4. Video classification va Image classificationning nima farqi bor?
- Image classificationda - Imagedagi objectning turini aniqlaymiz. It yoki mushuk! - static
- Video classificationdan - Videodagi harakatning turini. It saakrayabdimi? o'ynayabdimi? - Action

5. Image classification: Binary vs Multi
=> Binary classification — 2 ta sinf. Rasm shulardan faqat bittasiga tegishli.
Misol: olma yoki banan.
=> Multi-class classification — 3 yoki undan ko’p sinf. Rasm bittasiga tegishli.
Misol: Korean food’da kimchi, bibimbap, tteokbokki

                      Binary                 Multi-class
Sinflar soni	        2	                     K>=3
Chiqish neyronlari	  1	                     K
Activation	          sigmoid	               softmax
Loss	                Binary cross-entropy	 Categorical cross-entropy
